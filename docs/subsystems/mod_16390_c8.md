## Data and control flow

- `0x18b6f–0x18b79` copies the 12-byte `outro_text_params` rectangle into a local. The stack also holds three 8-byte bitmaps (emblem, background, panel). The emblem width and height are read at offsets `+0` and `+2` at `0x18bbb–0x18bc7`.
- For Orc credits, `0x18b84–0x18bed` loads group resource `0x1ce` and installs its bitmap in layer 15. The 18-byte layer record at `0x5ab9e` has an active word, four coordinate/dimension words, then bitmap and blitter pointers at `+0xa` and `+0xe`. Background/palette resources are `0x1cc/0x1cd` for Orc and `0x1ca/0x1cb` for Human (`0x18bf7–0x18c5e`).
- The signed upper halves of dword reads at `0x5ab7c` and `0x5ab80` supply the scroll Y and bottom extent: their sum must reach 31 (`0x18cbb–0x18cd2`). During text paging, Y instead targets `40 - font->rows`, where `rows` is the byte at font offset `+2` (`0x18dc4–0x18de3`). Tick comparisons zero-extend the word at `0x59436`; a tick of at least 2 advances the emblem animation, whose index wraps at 31 (`0x18cf9–0x18d3e`).
- The returned credits-page pointer is checked for NUL at `0x18db6` and `0x18e68`. After the closing text, `0x18eec–0x18efd` installs `OutroEnd` into three callback slots; the final event loop runs only while the word at `0x59430` is zero.
